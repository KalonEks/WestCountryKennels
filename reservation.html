Reservations

Book Your Stay
With Paw Partner.

For Boarding, we are now managing all of our reservations, bookings, and vaccinations through Paw Partner. Check out the free Paw Partner mobile app and create a profile for your pet.

Boarding reservations submitted online are considered requests only and are Not Confirmed until accepted by West Country Kennels. Once approved, your reservation status will change from Pending to Accepted.

Training reservations must be scheduled by phone so we can recommend the right program for your dog. This conversation ensures the goals for your dog are matched with the proper training program. Our team will also walk you through what to expect during the training process.

How to Register

Follow These Simple Steps

Use the Paw Partner app to create an account, manage vaccinations, and book your dog's stay.

Step 1 1

Create an Account

Start by downloading the Paw Partner app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Then create your account.

Step 2 2

Make a Reservation

After you create your account, click the "Make a Reservation" button.

Image placeholder
Step 3 3

Select West Country Kennels

Choose West Country Kennels from the list of boarding locations.

Image placeholder
Step 4 4

Complete Your Information

Fill in the rest of your information, such as contact information and address.

Image placeholder
Step 5 5

Complete Your Dog's Profile

Tell us about your dog so we can take the best care of them.

Image placeholder
Step 6 6

Choose a Reservation

Select which pet(s) you would like to make a reservation for, then enter your reservation details.

Image placeholder
Step 7 7

Upload Vaccination Records

We require all pets to be up-to-date on vaccinations before staying with us. Upload your documents through the app or on the Paw Partner website.

Image placeholder
Step 8 8

Pamper Your Pup

Let us know if you would like any additional services during their stay, such as a peaceful walk, grooming, one-on-one cuddle time, or a good game of fetch.

Image placeholder
Step 9 9

Confirm Your Reservation

After you complete your reservation details, West Country Kennels will contact you through the app and via email to confirm your reservation. You can check the status in the app.
