Gun-Dog Training Programs

We guide dogs through a structured progression. The Puppy Program lays the foundation for the Intermediate 4-Week, which is required before moving into the Intermediate Waterfowl or Intermediate 6-Week programs.

Bird & Gun Intro

PUPPY PROGRAM

Our 14-day Puppy Program introduces dogs to birds (including pigeons and chukars), firearms, and basic retrieving. Dogs 22 weeks and older, or those with no prior hunting experience, begin here. This program lays the essential foundation for a future hunting dog.

Pointing dogs may or may not display pointing instincts at this stage, and that's completely normal. It will be developed in the Intermediate programs.

Note: This program does not include obedience or E-collar.

$375 / week for 2 weeks

Includes birds

INTERMEDIATE

Our 28-day program establishes the core foundation for Pointers and Retrievers, developing confidence, trust, control, and focus in the field.

$375 / week for 4 or 6 weeks
  • On Leash Obedience (Heel, Sit, Stay, Kennel Up, Down, Off, and No)
  • E-collar Training (Coming When Called, Here Command, and Off Leash Control Applicable for Field and Home Environments)
  • Field Skills (Flushing, Pointing for Pointing Breeds, Tracking, Quartering)
  • Retrieving (Introduction to Basic Retrieving Skills; Not Retrieve-To-Hand)
  • Includes Birds

Prerequisite: Puppy Program

ADVANCED

Our 42-day program expands on the four-week course, advancing your dog's obedience, field experience and introducing Retrieve to Hand.
