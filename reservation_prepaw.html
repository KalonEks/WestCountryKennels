How to Reserve

Follow These Simple Steps

Call our team to request boarding or training and we will guide you through the full process.

Step 1 1

Call West Country Kennels

Call us at (320) 544-0732 to start your reservation request.

Step 2 2

Discuss Your Dog and Dates

Tell us about your dog, preferred dates, and the type of stay you need.

Step 3 3

Choose a Service

We will help you choose boarding, board and train, or a training program.

Step 4 4

Submit Reservation Details

Share owner contact details, emergency contact info, and any care instructions.

Step 5 5

Provide Vaccination Records

Send current Rabies, DHP-P, and Bordetella records before your arrival date.

Step 6 6

Review Deposits and Policies

We will confirm any required deposits, cancellation windows, and check-in details.

Step 7 7

Prepare for Drop-Off

Bring food instructions, medications, and any requested paperwork on arrival day.
