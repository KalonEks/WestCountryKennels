Comfortable Stays

Boarding That
Feels Like Home

West Country Kennels is a home away from home for any and all dogs. Whether you're looking for short-term or long-term boarding, our facility provides a safe, comfortable, and engaging environment where your dog will feel just as loved as you love them!

VACCINATIONS: For any dog staying at our facility, we require Rabies, DHP-P, and Bordetella vaccines to be current. Please upload your current vaccines into our mobile app Paw Partner, and confirm that they are current before your dog arrives at our facility.

BOARDING DEPOSITS: A 2-day deposit is required for holiday and peak-event reservations (New Year's, March, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas). A 1-day deposit is required for reservations from May through August. Holiday deposits are refundable with 14+ days' notice; May-August deposits are refundable with 7+ days' notice. Deposits are forfeited if canceled within the respective windows.

Board & Train

Board and Train

Our Board and Train option provides added time with a trainer to reinforce past training or give your dog extra fun, structured one-on-one time with a staff member during their boarding stay. These brush-up or engagement sessions can be added for the full stay, part of the visit, or even just a single day. Dogs with prior training will receive obedience work, with field work including birds will be incorporated when their previous experience makes it appropriate.

$85 / day
  • One-On-One Sessions
  • Training or Engagement Options
  • Brush Intermediate Skills When Applicable
